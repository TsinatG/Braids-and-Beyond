Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend booking your appointment at least 1-2 weeks in advance, especially for more complex styles or during busy seasons. This ensures you get your preferred stylist and time slot. For last-minute appointments, you can always call us to check for availability.

Most braided styles can last between 4-8 weeks depending on the style, your hair type, and how well you maintain them. Box braids and knotless braids typically last 6-8 weeks, while styles like cornrows may last 2-4 weeks. We'll provide you with care instructions to help your style last as long as possible.

Yes, we offer high-quality hair extensions for all our braiding services. You can choose from our selection of premium synthetic and human hair options in various colors and textures. If you prefer to bring your own hair extensions, that's perfectly fine too. Just make sure they're pre-stretched and ready for installation.

We understand that plans can change. We request that you notify us at least 24 hours in advance if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before your scheduled appointment may incur a cancellation fee of 25% of the service price. No-shows will be charged 50% of the service price.

For the best results, please come with freshly washed and deep conditioned hair. Your hair should be completely dry and detangled. Avoid using heavy oils or products before your appointment as they can make the braiding process more difficult. If you have any specific questions about preparing your hair for your chosen style, feel free to contact us before your appointment.
