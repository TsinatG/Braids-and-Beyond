About GLAM Salon

Founded in 2010, GLAM Salon has been providing premium hair styling services to New York City's fashion-forward clientele for over a decade.

Our mission is to help every client express their unique personality through customized hair styling that enhances their natural beauty and fits their lifestyle.

Our Story

GLAM Salon was founded by award-winning stylist Jane Doe, who wanted to create a space where creativity and technical excellence could flourish. After working in some of the most prestigious salons in Paris and London, Jane brought her vision to New York City.

What started as a small studio with just two chairs has grown into one of the most sought-after salons in the city, known for innovative techniques and personalized service.

Our Team

Our team of stylists comes from diverse backgrounds but shares a common passion for hair and continuous education. Each stylist undergoes rigorous training and stays current with the latest trends and techniques.

We believe in collaboration and often work together to achieve the perfect look for our clients, combining different perspectives and specialties.

Our Approach

At GLAM, we believe that great hair starts with understanding. Every appointment begins with a thorough consultation to understand your hair goals, lifestyle, and maintenance preferences.

We take into account your face shape, hair texture, and personal style to create a look that's uniquely yours and easy to maintain between visits.

Our commitment to quality extends to the products we use. We've carefully selected professional-grade, environmentally conscious products that deliver exceptional results.
